# Inventory Management System

## 📦 Project Overview

The Inventory Management System is a web-based application developed to manage products, suppliers, purchases, sales, stock levels, and inventory reports.

The system helps users maintain inventory records efficiently and provides automatic stock updates when products are purchased or sold.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To manage product information.
- To manage supplier information.
- To record product purchases.
- To record product sales.
- To automatically update stock quantity.
- To identify low-stock products.
- To generate inventory reports.
- To provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 1. Login System
- User login with username and password.
- Session-based authentication.

### 2. Dashboard
- Total products.
- Total suppliers.
- Total stock.
- Low-stock products.
- Quick access to system modules.

### 3. Product Management
- Add products.
- Edit products.
- Delete products.
- Search products.
- Track product quantity.
- Set minimum stock level.

### 4. Supplier Management
- Add suppliers.
- Edit supplier information.
- Delete suppliers.
- View supplier details.

### 5. Purchase Management
- Select supplier.
- Select product.
- Enter purchase quantity.
- Enter purchase price.
- Automatically increase product stock.
- Maintain purchase records.

### 6. Sales Management
- Select product.
- Enter sale quantity.
- Enter selling price.
- Check available stock.
- Automatically decrease product stock.
- Prevent sales when stock is insufficient.

### 7. Reports
- Total products report.
- Total stock report.
- Low-stock report.
- Purchase report.
- Sales report.
- Total purchase amount.
- Total sales amount.
